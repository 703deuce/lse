Google policy

Ask for Reviews the Right Way

Local SEO Express helps you request honest reviews. It does not create fake reviews or promise only positive feedback.

Google allows businesses to remind customers to leave reviews and share review links or QR codes. Google does not allow businesses to offer free or discounted products or services in exchange for reviews.

⏱

Ask real customers

🔗

Do not buy reviews

🎨

No rewards for reviews

Do not offer discounts or gifts in exchange for a review or rating.

👥

Fair opportunity for feedback

Do not hide the Google review option from unhappy customers.
