# Local SEO Express — Full Feature List

Every product page/feature, what it does, and why it matters for the customer.

## Getting started & account

**Onboarding** — Guided setup to create/connect a business and start using the product.  
Gets a new user to a first win fast.

**Overview / Dashboard** — Snapshot of rankings, reviews, audits, and next actions for one location.  
Shows “how am I doing?” in one screen.

**Business Profile** — Edit business name, address, phone, Google Place, keywords, and location settings.  
Keeps every tool pointed at the right business.

**Billing** — Plan, usage, and upgrade options.  
Shows what they have unlocked and what to buy next.

**Settings / Profile / Team / Security** — Account identity, invites, password/session controls.  
Lets owners manage access safely.

**Branding** *(agency)* — Logo, colors, and report footer for white-label deliverables.  
Makes client reports look like the agency’s brand.

## Reviews & reputation

**Send Request** — Send one SMS or email asking a customer for a Google review.  
Turns happy customers into reviews without awkward in-person asks.

**Customers / Contacts** — Customer list with eligibility, request history, import, and export.  
Knows who to ask next and avoids duplicate spam.

**Campaigns** — Multi-step review automations (SMS/email + waits + follow-ups).  
Keeps asking automatically until they click, reply, or opt out.

**Request History** — Log of past review requests and delivery outcomes.  
Proof of who was asked and what happened.

**QR Code & Review Link** — Create tracked Google review links and printable QR posters.  
Collects reviews offline (counter, invoice, car wrap) with measurable scans.

**Templates** — Reusable SMS/email review-request messages with merge fields.  
Consistent, on-brand asks every time.

**Reviews inbox** — See Google reviews and draft replies in one place.  
Respond faster and stay on top of reputation.

**Review Velocity** — Charts for review volume, speed, and trust trends.  
Shows if review growth is accelerating or stalling.

**Competitor Reviews** — Compare review counts/ratings/themes vs competitors.  
Shows who is winning the review race locally.

**Review Insights** — Themes and keywords customers mention in reviews.  
Turns feedback into product/service improvement priorities.

**Reputation Overview** — High-level review health + coach signals.  
Quick “are we on track?” for reputation.

**Reputation Audit** — Strategic reputation report for a location.  
Client-ready diagnosis of review strength.

**Automations** — Webhooks/triggers so jobs from Zapier/Make/CRM enroll into campaigns.  
Starts review requests automatically when work is completed.

**Alerts** — Notifications for reputation risks and opportunities.  
Warns when volume drops or negatives spike.

**Review Sites / Reputation Settings** — Review link, quiet hours, detection, retention, sender settings.  
Configures how review collection works for that location.

## Text messaging (Twilio ISV / A2P)

**Messaging Overview** — Status of SMS readiness for the business.  
Shows whether they can legally/reliably send texts yet.

**A2P Business Details** — Collect legal business info for carrier registration.  
Required to get approved for business SMS.

**A2P Use Case** — Describe SMS purpose, samples, and compliance language.  
Satisfies Twilio/carrier 10DLC review.

**A2P Review & Submit** — Final check before filing registration.  
Reduces failed submissions.

**A2P Status** — Live Brand/Campaign approval progress.  
Tracks “are we approved yet?”

**Phone Number** — Search, buy, and manage a dedicated sending number.  
Sends from a real business number instead of a random shared line.

## Rank tracking (Google Maps)

**New Scan / Maps Scans** — Run a GeoGrid scan of Google Maps rankings across a service area.  
Proves where they show up on the map pack, not just one search.

**Scan Grid Result** — Pin-by-pin map of ranks for a keyword.  
Visual proof of coverage gaps and wins.

**Scan History** — Past scans across the account.  
Reopen old proof for meetings and comparisons.

**Keywords / Maps Campaigns** — Group keywords and schedule recurring Maps scans.  
Tracks rankings over time without redoing setup every week.

**Keyword Tracker** — Track individual keyword ranks and opportunities.  
Helps decide what terms matter most to own.

## Local SEO audits & growth tools

**Health Assessment** — Fast local SEO health check for a location/keyword.  
Quick “what’s wrong” without a full deep audit.

**Complete Audit / Growth Audit** — Full audit across GBP, website, coverage, and competitors.  
Prioritized diagnosis of what is hurting local visibility.

**Growth Plan** — Action list generated from the audit.  
Clear fix list instead of a dense report nobody reads.

**Backlink Gap** — Find linking domains competitors have that you don’t.  
Shows real outreach targets for stronger local authority.

**Local Trust** — Local credibility opportunities (directories, mentions, trust signals).  
Finds easy wins that make the business look more legitimate.

**AI Visibility** — Checks whether AI engines mention the business for local prompts.  
Tracks presence in the new AI-search world.

**AI Prompts** — Manage the questions used for AI visibility checks.  
Controls what gets tested.

**Citations** — NAP consistency / citation checks.  
Fixes address/phone mismatches that confuse Google and customers.

**Prospects** *(agency)* — Pipeline of potential clients.

**Prospect Audits** *(agency)* — Run/share audits before closing a deal.

**Clients** *(agency)* — List of managed client locations.
